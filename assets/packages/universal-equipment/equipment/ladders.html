--- { "name": "2x Ladders" } --- Ladders are Insignificant and Exposed terrain. Before the battle, you can set up any of them as follows:
  1. Wholly within your territory.
  2. Upright against terrain that's at least 2" tall.
  3. More than 2" from other equipment terrain features.
  4. More than 1" from doors and access points.
Once per action, whenever an operative is climbing the terrain feature a ladder is placed against, treat the vertical distance as 1" if the ladder is within that operative's control range during that entire climb.
