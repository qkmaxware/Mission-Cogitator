--- { "name": "Grudge", "art-path": "assets/packages/teams/hearthkyn-salvagers/effects/hearthkyn-salvagers-grudge-token.svg" } --- Whenever an opposing HEARTHKYN SALVAGER operative is shooting against, fighting against or retaliating against an this operative, for each of their Grudge tokens that this operative has, the opposing HEARTHKYN SALVAGER can retain one of their normal successes as a critical success instead (including any normal successes already retained as a result of the Accurate weapon rule). Grudge tokens are not removed when this happens.
