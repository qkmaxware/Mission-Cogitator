--- { "name": "Network Counteract" } ---

This rule is a part of the Noospheric Network faction rule.

After a friendly Battleclade Servitor has transfered power to another operator that operator then proceeds to act as soon as the transferer's activation is done. The following actions are available to the newly activated operator:

  • Chooses its order: Conceal order or Engage order
  • Perform one 1 AP action for free
  • Cannot move moew than 2" during the activation
  • An operative that does NETWORK COUNTERACT cannot do so again, or counteract, during the same turning point.
