--- { "kind": "strategy", "name": "Clearance Sweep", "art-path": null, "art-caption": null } --- Place your Clearance Sweep marker in the killzone. Whenever a friendly KASRKIN operative within 5" horizontally of that marker is shooting an operative also within 5" horizontally of that marker, that friendly operative's weapons have the Ceaseless weapon rule. In the Ready step of the next Strategy Phase, remove that marker.
