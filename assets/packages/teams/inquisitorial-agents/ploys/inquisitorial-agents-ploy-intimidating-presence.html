--- { "kind": "firefight", "name": "Intimidating Presence", "art-path": null, "art-caption": null } --- Use this firefight ploy when an enemy operative Visible to and within 3" of a friendly INQUISITORIAL AGENT operative, or Visible to and within 6" of a friendly MYSTIC operative, performs the Pick Up Marker or a mission action (excluding Operate Hatch). Your opponent must spend 1 additional AP for that enemy operative to perform that action (if they cannot or choose not to, the AP spent on it is refunded).
