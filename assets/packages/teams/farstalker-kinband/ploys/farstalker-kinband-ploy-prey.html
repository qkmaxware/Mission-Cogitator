--- { "kind": "strategy", "name": "Prey", "art-path": null, "art-caption": null } --- Whenever a friendly FARSTALKER KINBAND operative is shooting during an Activation in which it hasn't performed the Charge, Fall Back or Reposition action, its ranged weapons have the Balanced and Severe weapon rules; if the weapon already has the Balanced weapon rule, it has the Ceaseless and Severe weapon rules instead. Note that operative isn't restricted from performing those actions after shooting.
