--- { "name": "Toxin Shot", "art-path": null, "art-caption": null } --- Once per turning point, when a friendly FARSTALKER KINBAND operative is performing the Shoot action and you select a Kroot rifle, Kroot scattergun or dual Kroot pistols (focused), you can use this rule. If you do, until the end of that action, that weapon has the Lethal 5+ and Stun weapon rules. You cannot use the Piercing Shot and Toxin Shot rule during the same action.
