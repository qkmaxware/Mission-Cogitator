  • Select one enemy operative within this operative’s Control Range to receives your Apprehended Token. Until that enemy operative is no longer within this operative’s Control Range, or until this operative performs this Action again (whichever comes first), worsen the Hit stat of that enemy operative’s weapons by 1 (this isn’t cumulative with being Injured); in addition, that enemy operative cannot perform the Fall Back action.
  • This operative cannot perform this action unless an enemy operative is within its Control Range.
