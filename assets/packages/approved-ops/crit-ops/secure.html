--- { "name": "Secure", "kind": "primary", "archetype": "fixed", } --- Victory Points:

At the end of each turning point after the first you get 1VP for any objective markers secured by your team and if you have more markers than your opponent then you score 1 additional VP.

Mission Action:
Secure (1AP)

  • One objective marker the active operative controls is secured by your kill team until the enemy kill team secures that objective marker.
  • An operative cannot perform this action during the first turning point, or while within control range of an enemy operative.
