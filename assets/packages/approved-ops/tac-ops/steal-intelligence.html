--- { "name": "Steal Intelligence", "kind": "secondary", "archetype": "infiltration", } --- Reveal:

The first time an enemy operative is incapacitated.

Additional Rules:

Whenever an enemy operative is incapacitated, before it's removed from the killzone, place one of your intelligence mission markers within it's Control Range.

Friendly operatives can perform the Pick up Marker acton on your intelligence mission markers, and for the purpsoe of that action's conditions, ignore the first intelligence mission marker the acgive operative is carrying. In other words, each friendly operative can carry up to two intelligence mission markers, or one and any other marker.

Victory Points:

At the end of each turning point after the first, if any friendly operatives are carrying any of your intelligence mission markers, you score 1VP.

At the end of the battle, for each of your intelligence mission markers friendly operatives are carrying, you score 1VP.
