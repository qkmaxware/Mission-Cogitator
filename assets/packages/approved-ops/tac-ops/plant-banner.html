When you perform the Plant Banner action

Victory Points:

At the end of each turning point after the first, if your Banner mission marker is wholly within your opponent's territory and friendly operatives control it, you score 1VP; if that's true and no enemy operatives contest it, you score 2VP instead. Your banner mission marker must be in the killzone to score (not being carried).
