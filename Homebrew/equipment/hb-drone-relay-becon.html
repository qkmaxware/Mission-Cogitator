--- { "name": "Drone Relay Beacon" } ---
The beacon does not improve the marksman. It simply ensures the enemy is exactly where the marksman expects them to be.

Once per Turning Point, when a friendly operative within 6" of this operative performs a Shoot action, you can activate the Drone Relay Beacon.

Until the end of that shooting attack, ignore the target being Obscured (but not Concealed).
