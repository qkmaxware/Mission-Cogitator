--- { "name": "Breach and Clear", "art-path": null, "art-caption": null } --- Once per turning point, when a ready friendly IMPERIAL NAVY BREACHER operative is activated, you can use this rule. If you do, select one other ready friendly IMPERIAL NAVY BREACHER operative Visible to and within 3" of that operative. When that first friendly operative is expended, you can activate that other friendly operative before your opponent activates. When that other operative is expended, your opponent then activates as normal.
