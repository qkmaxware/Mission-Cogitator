--- { "name": "Shock Assault", "kind": "firefight" } --- Use this firefight ploy when a friendly ANGEL OF DEATH operative is performing the Fight action during an activation in which it performed the Charge action, at the start of the Resolve Attack Dice step. Until the end of that action:
  • Its melee weapon has the Shock weapon rule.
  • The first time you strike during that sequence, inflict 1 additional damage (to a maximum of 7).
