--- { "kind": "strategy", "name": "Relocate", "art-path": null, "art-caption": null } --- Select one friendly KASRKIN operative that’s more than 3" from enemy operatives. That operative, and each other friendly KASRKIN operative that’s both within 3" of that operative and more than 3" from enemy operatives, can immediately perform a free Dash action in an order of your choice. You cannot use this ploy during the first turning point.
