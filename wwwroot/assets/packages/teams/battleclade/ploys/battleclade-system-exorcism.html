--- { "name": "System Exorcism", "kind": "firefight" } --- Use this firefight ploy when you would activate a friendly BATTLECLADE operative. Remove one rules effect or stat change your opponent has applied to it (e.g. Poison token, -1APL, cannot be activated or perform actions, etc.), then activate it. This ploy cannot allow it to regain lost wounds, ignore the effects of being injured or remove mission pack rules.
