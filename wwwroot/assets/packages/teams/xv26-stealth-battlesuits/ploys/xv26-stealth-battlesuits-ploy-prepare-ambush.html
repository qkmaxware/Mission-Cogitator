--- { "kind": "strategy", "name": "Prepare Ambush", "art-path": null, "art-caption": null } --- Place one of your Ambush markers wholly within your territory and more than 2" from enemy operatives. Whenever a friendly XV26 STEALTH BATTLESUIT operative is shooting an enemy operative that's within 2" of that marker, you can use this rule. If you do, that friendly operative's ranged weapons have the Seek weapon rule until the end of the Action. Remove that Ambush marker at the end of that Action or in the Ready step of the next turning point (whichever comes first).
