--- { "name": "Burrowing Strike", "kind": "firefight" } --- Use this firefight ploy when a friendly RAVENER operative performs the Burrow action. Before that operative is removed from the killzone, or after setting it up on your TUNNEL, inflict D3+1 damage on each enemy operative within its control range (roll separately for each). You cannot use this ploy in the Strategy phase, or during a FELLTALON operative's activation or counteraction if it performs the Toxic Lunge action (and vice versa).
