--- { "name": "Damage" } --- Operatives have a Wounds stat - the number of wounds they have. Damage inflicted reduces their wounds accordingly. Operatives with fewer that their starting wounds are wounded. Operatives with fewer than half of their starting wounds are injured: subtract 2\" from their Move stat and worsen the Hit stat of their weapons by 1. Operatives with wounds of 0 or less are incapacitated and removed.
