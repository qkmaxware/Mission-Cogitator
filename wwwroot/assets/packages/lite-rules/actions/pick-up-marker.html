--- { "name": "Pick Up Marker", "ap": 1 } --- A universal action that allows an operative to remove a valid marker it controls from the killzone and begin carrying it. Once carrying the marker, the operative is considered to be carrying, contesting, and controlling that marker.
  • The operative must control the marker.
  • The marker must be one that the mission or another rule specifically says can be picked up.
  • The operative cannot be within Control Range of an enemy operative.
  • The operative cannot already be carrying a marker.
